### **Understanding the Problem**  
We need to find:  
1. The **pH** of a **0.0335 M methylamine (CH₃NH₂)** solution.  
2. The equilibrium concentrations of **CH₃NH₂** (base) and **CH₃NH₃⁺** (its conjugate acid).  

**Given:**  
- \( K_b = 4.47 \times 10^{-4} \) (base dissociation constant).  
- Methylamine is a weak base:  
  \[
  \text{CH₃NH₂} + \text{H₂O} \leftrightharpoons \text{CH₃NH₃⁺} + \text{OH}^-
  \]  

---

### **Step 1: Set Up the ICE Table**  

| **Species** | **Initial (M)** | **Change (M)** | **Equilibrium (M)** |  
|-------------|------------------|-----------------|----------------------|  
| CH₃NH₂      | 0.0335           | -x              | \( 0.0335 - x \)     |  
| CH₃NH₃⁺     | 0                | +x              | \( x \)              |  
| OH⁻         | 0                | +x              | \( x \)              |  

---

### **Step 2: Write the \( K_b \) Expression**  
\[
K_b = \frac{[\text{CH₃NH₃⁺}][\text{OH}^-]}{[\text{CH₃NH₂}]} = \frac{x^2}{0.0335 - x}
\]  

---

### **Step 3: Check the "x is small" Assumption**  
Assume \( x \ll 0.0335 \):  
\[
x^2 \approx K_b \cdot 0.0335 = (4.47 \times 10^{-4})(0.0335) = 1.498 \times 10^{-5}  
\]
\[
x \approx \sqrt{1.498 \times 10^{-5}} \approx 0.00387 \, \text{M}  
\]  

**Check the 5% Rule:**  
\[
\frac{x}{0.0335} = \frac{0.00387}{0.0335} \approx 0.1155 \, (\text{11.55\%})  
\]  
Since **11.55% > 5%**, the assumption **fails**. Use the quadratic formula!  

---

### **Step 4: Solve the Quadratic Equation**  
Rewrite the \( K_b \) expression:  
\[
x^2 + (4.47 \times 10^{-4})x - (4.47 \times 10^{-4} \cdot 0.0335) = 0  
\]  
\[
x^2 + 0.000447x - 0.00001498 = 0  
\]  

**Quadratic Formula:**  
\[
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \quad \text{where } a=1, \, b=0.000447, \, c=-0.00001498  
\]  
\[
x = \frac{-0.000447 \pm \sqrt{(0.000447)^2 + 4(1)(0.00001498)}}{2}  
\]  
\[
x \approx 0.00365 \, \text{M} \quad (\text{only positive root})  
\]  

---

### **Step 5: Calculate pH and Concentrations**  

1. **[OH⁻] = 0.00365 M**  
   \[
   \text{pOH} = -\log(0.00365) \approx 2.44 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \text{pH} = 14 - 2.44 = \boxed{11.56}  
   \]  

2. **[CH₃NH₂]** (equilibrium):  
   \[
   0.0335 - x = 0.0335 - 0.00365 = \boxed{0.0298 \, \text{M}}  
   \]  

3. **[CH₃NH₃⁺]** (equilibrium):  
   \[
   x = \boxed{0.00365 \, \text{M}}  
   \]  

---

### **Key Takeaways**  
- **Assumption Check:** Always verify if \( x \) is small using the 5% rule.  
- **Why Quadratic?** For weak bases with low concentration or high \( K_b \), approximations often fail.  

**Final Answer:**  
- pH = **11.56**  
- [CH₃NH₂] = **0.0298 M**  
- [CH₃NH₃⁺] = **0.00365 M**
